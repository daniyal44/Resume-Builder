The eight resume templates

Each layout uses the same underlying content, so you can switch template at any point without retyping anything. Styles range from conservative single-column documents to modern two-column designs.

1. Classic

Centred header, rule-separated sections, serif-free and completely single-column. The safest choice for large employers, government roles and any applicant tracking system.

2. Modern Sidebar

A left contact rail with skills and languages, plus a wide main column for experience. Great for product, marketing and design roles where a little personality helps.

3. Bold Header

Large name block, strong section labels and confident spacing. Works well for sales, leadership and customer-facing positions that need immediate impact.

4. Professional Split

Balanced two-column layout with experience on the left and education, skills and certifications on the right. Good for mid-career applicants with a lot to show.

5. Minimal

A narrow label column and generous whitespace. Quietly elegant, very readable, and excellent when your achievements speak for themselves.

6. Elegant Minimal

Refined typography with light rules and subtle emphasis. Ideal for consulting, finance, legal and academic CVs where restraint reads as credibility.

7. Creative Grid

A modular grid that groups projects, skills and interests visually. Best for designers, developers and creatives applying to modern teams.

8. Pikachu

Dark right-hand sidebar with an inverted palette. A bold option for startups and creative agencies — still exports real, selectable text.

Every template exports as real text Nothing is flattened into an image, so parsers can read your name, employers, dates and skills the same way a human does.

How to choose the right resume template

The best template is the one that makes your experience easiest to scan in six seconds. Use these rules of thumb instead of picking purely on looks:

  • Applying through a job board or a large company? Choose Classic, Minimal or Elegant Minimal. Single-column order is unambiguous for automated screening.
  • Applying to a startup, agency or creative team? Modern Sidebar, Creative Grid and Pikachu show personality without hurting readability.
  • Career changer with projects? Professional Split and Creative Grid give projects their own visual weight.
  • Senior or executive? Bold Header and Elegant Minimal handle long achievement lists without looking cramped.
  • Academic or research CV? Minimal extends cleanly over multiple pages; keep section labels and reverse-chronological order.

Customising colour and fonts

Every template supports an accent colour, so you can match a company brand, an industry norm or simply your personal preference. A few practical limits:

  • Keep to one accent colour — two competing colours make a resume read as a flyer.
  • Navy, charcoal and deep teal are safe in every industry; bright pink and neon suit creative roles only.
  • Body text should stay near-black on white for contrast and photocopy legibility.
  • Keep resumes to one page below ten years of experience, two pages beyond that.

Frequently asked questions

Are these resume templates really free?

Yes. All eight templates are free to use, with no watermark, no sign-up and no export limit. You can create and download as many resumes as you like.

Which resume template is best for ATS?

Single-column layouts such as Classic, Minimal and Elegant Minimal are safest because text is read top to bottom. Two-column templates still export real text and work with most modern systems.

Can I edit a template without paying?

Yes. Every template is edited in the browser with a live A4 preview, then exported as a PDF. There is no payment step and no account.

Do the templates work for a CV as well as a resume?

Yes. The layouts accept any amount of experience, education, projects and skills, so the same template works for a one-page resume or a longer academic CV.
